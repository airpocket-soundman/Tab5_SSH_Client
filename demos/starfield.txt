starfield.py - graphical starfield demo

Run:
  python /starfield.py [wait_ms] [frames] [speed]

Arguments:
  wait_ms   Delay after each frame in milliseconds. Default: 0.
  frames    Number of frames to draw. Default: 30.
  speed     Star movement speed. Default: 5.

Controls:
  Ctrl-C or q stops the demo.

Notes:
  Stars move from the center outward. Each frame clears the graphics area,
  draws short star trails, then presents the sprite.

Examples:
  python /starfield.py
  python /starfield.py 10 200 7
