life.py - Conway's Game of Life demo

Run:
  python /life.py [wait_ms] [frames] [cell_px]

Arguments:
  wait_ms   Delay after each frame in milliseconds. Default: 0.
  frames    Maximum number of generations to draw. Default: 200.
  cell_px   Cell size in pixels. Default: 12. Minimum: 6.

Controls:
  Ctrl-C or q stops the demo.

Notes:
  The board is fixed at 25x25 cells and is drawn through gfx.mono(), a packed
  1-bit bitmap drawing command. This keeps the MicroPython side small and lets
  the firmware draw the board into the M5GFX sprite more quickly.

Examples:
  python /life.py
  python /life.py 20
  python /life.py 0 500 10
